Building WebSocket Chat with Go

M
Mohammed El Bouzi @mohammed
📅 Posted 2 hours ago
Programming

Today I implemented real time communication using Go and WebSocket. The experience was incredible — the power of goroutines makes concurrent connections seamless. I've been working on this for the past few weeks and I'm excited to share what I've learned.

WebSockets provide full-duplex communication channels over a single TCP connection. Combined with Go's lightweight goroutines, you can handle thousands of concurrent connections with minimal overhead.

🖼️ WebSocket Architecture Diagram

The architecture is straightforward: a Hub manages all connected clients, each client runs in its own goroutine, and messages are broadcast through channels.

One of the biggest challenges was handling graceful disconnection and reconnection. I implemented a ping/pong mechanism to detect stale connections.
